# Privacy Policy — AquApp

*Last updated: October 16, 2026*

> Version française : [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md)

---

## In short

**AquApp has no account, no server, no ads and no analytics.** Your data stays on your iPhone. The only information that leaves the device is an **approximate location** (to within about 1 km), sent to Apple's weather service to adjust your goal during hot weather, and only if you allow location access.

---

## Health notice

AquApp provides **general guidelines**. The hydration goal, alcohol compensation and heatwave bonus are estimates based on simple formulas. They are not a diagnosis or medical advice, and they don't replace advice from a health professional.

If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, take medication, have a heart or kidney condition, or need to limit your fluid intake, ask your doctor before following these goals.

Alcohol abuse is dangerous for your health. Please drink responsibly.

---

## Data stored on your iPhone

It is kept **only on your iPhone**, in the app's local database, the iOS Keychain and the app's settings:

- **Body profile**: weight, height and sex, to calculate your goal
- **First name**, to personalise the app
- **History**: amounts of water and alcoholic drinks, with their time
- **Progress**: streaks, achievements, challenges, XP and running totals
- **Preferences**: goal, reminders, theme, icon, badge and custom drinks
- **Profile photo**, if you choose one
- **Premium status**

---

## Retention

Without Premium, entries older than 30 days are deleted automatically. With Premium, your history is kept while the app is installed, or until you delete it.

---

## Apple Health (optional)

AquApp offers to connect Apple Health during setup; you can decline.

- **Writing**: the water you log in AquApp is added to Health.
- **Reading**: your **steps**, **workouts**, **sleep** and the **water** logged in Health, only to validate some challenges and achievements.

This data is used **on your iPhone**: it is never sent anywhere else or used for advertising. You can change these permissions at any time in *Settings › Health › Data Access*.

---

## Data that leaves your iPhone

- **Weather (optional)**: if you allow location access, your position **rounded to about 1 km** is sent to **Apple's WeatherKit** service to get the local temperature. Apple uses this position only to provide the weather, without linking it to your identity. No identifier or health data is sent with it.
- **Siri and Shortcuts**: voice commands are processed by Apple under its own privacy policy. AquApp only receives the requested action, for example “add 250 ml of water”.
- **Purchases**: they are handled by Apple's App Store. AquApp receives no payment information.

---

## Why this data is used

- **Running the app** (profile, history, progress, settings): needed to provide the service you use (GDPR, Art. 6(1)(b)).
- **Health data** (Apple Health, water and alcohol intake): your **explicit consent** (Art. 9(2)(a)), given in the iOS permission prompt and revocable at any time.
- **Approximate location**: your **consent** (Art. 6(1)(a)), given in the iOS location prompt and revocable in *Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services*.
- **Reminders**: your consent, through the notification permission. They are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone.

---

## What we don't do

- No account, no email address required
- No server owned by AquApp
- No analytics, audience measurement or advertising tools
- No cross-app tracking, no advertising identifier
- No sale or sharing of your data

---

## Minimum age

AquApp is intended for people aged **15 and over**, the age from which you can consent to the processing of your data on your own in France. Alcohol-related features are intended for people of legal drinking age in their country.

---

## Your rights

You can access, correct, erase and restrict the use of your data, and withdraw your consent at any time.

- **Access and correction**: all your data is visible and editable in the app.
- **Erasure**: *Profile › Privacy › Delete my data* erases everything AquApp keeps on your iPhone in one go, including the Keychain, which iOS keeps even after the app is deleted. You can also erase the water AquApp logged in Health.
- **Complaint**: if you believe your rights are not respected, you can contact the French data protection authority, the **CNIL** ([cnil.fr](https://www.cnil.fr)), or the data protection authority in your country.
